Suggested Twilio message flow:
End users opt in by visiting https://antoniofordistrict5.com/sms-updates/, entering their mobile number, and actively checking an unchecked consent box agreeing to receive recurring political campaign text messages from Committee to Elect Antonio Baciu for City Council District 5 '26. The disclosure states that message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and users can reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of donation or support. SMS Terms: https://antoniofordistrict5.com/sms-terms/. Privacy Policy: https://antoniofordistrict5.com/privacy-policy/.

Important: This website opt-in does not create consent for phone numbers obtained from a voter file or other third-party list. Only contacts who actually complete the opt-in flow should be described to Twilio as having opted in through this website.

Once Twilio A2P registration is approved, configure Twilio's default or Advanced Opt-Out handling and the required opt-in confirmation before recurring sends.
