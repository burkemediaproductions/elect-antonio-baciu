Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026.

This website is operated for the Committee to Elect Antonio Baciu for City Council District 5 '26. This Privacy Policy explains how the campaign collects, uses, and protects information submitted by website visitors, supporters, volunteers, endorsers, donors, and people who choose to receive campaign communications.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the campaign, we may collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, mailing address, volunteer interests, endorsement information, messages you submit, and donation-related information required for campaign finance compliance.

How We Use Information

We may use information you provide to respond to inquiries, administer campaign forms, process support requests, coordinate volunteers and endorsements, communicate campaign updates, maintain records, improve website operations, and comply with applicable campaign-finance or legal obligations.

SMS / Text Messaging Privacy

If you expressly opt in to receive text messages from Committee to Elect Antonio Baciu for City Council District 5 '26, we may use the mobile number you provide to send recurring campaign text messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be disclosed to service providers that help us deliver campaign communications, but only as necessary to provide those services and subject to appropriate restrictions.

You may opt out of campaign text messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact the campaign at info@antoniofordistrict5.com or +1-760-808-1014. See our SMS Terms & Conditions.

Donations and Payment Processing

Donation payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. The campaign does not use this website to store full credit card numbers. Campaign contributions may require collection and reporting of information under applicable campaign-finance laws.

Website Hosting, Forms, Analytics, and Service Providers

The campaign may use website hosting, form-processing, analytics, security, email, messaging, payment, and other service providers to operate the website and campaign communications. These providers may process information on the campaign's behalf as necessary to provide their services.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for security, functionality, analytics, and performance. Your browser may allow you to limit or disable cookies.

Data Retention and Security

The campaign uses reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, legal obligations, recordkeeping, or dispute resolution.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional information. You may unsubscribe from campaign email using the unsubscribe method included in those messages. You may opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP.

External Links

This website may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to info@antoniofordistrict5.com, by phone at +1-760-808-1014, or by mail to Committee to Elect Antonio Baciu for City Council District 5 '26, 27765 Landau Blvd. Suite 104, PMB #20, Cathedral City, CA 92234.
