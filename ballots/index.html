Four priorities

Practical Focus for District 5

Antonio's campaign centers on everyday city issues that affect residents, neighborhoods, and the quality of life in Cathedral City.

01

Public Safety

Support strong police and fire services and keep neighborhood safety at the center of City decisions.

02

Better Roads

Keep attention on streets, infrastructure, and the day-to-day improvements residents can see and use.

03

Responsible Spending

Approach public dollars carefully, prioritize core services, and focus resources on measurable community needs.

04

Community Engagement

Stay accessible, listen to residents, and strengthen the connection between City Hall and District 5 neighborhoods.

Service & support

Rooted in Cathedral City

Antonio serves as Vice Chair of the Parks & Community Events Commission and as a member of the Finance Advisory Committee. His campaign has also received support from local public safety organizations and community leaders.
