SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 6, 2026.

These SMS Terms & Conditions govern recurring text messages from Committee to Elect Antonio Baciu for City Council District 5 '26 (the "Campaign").

Program Description

People who expressly opt in may receive recurring political campaign text messages from the Campaign, which may include campaign news, event information, volunteer opportunities, fundraising or donation requests, election-related reminders, and links to campaign content.

Consent

By providing your mobile number and actively checking the SMS consent box on the Campaign's text updates form, you consent to receive recurring text messages from the Campaign at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of making a donation, purchasing anything, or otherwise supporting the Campaign.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for applicable rates.

Opt Out

Reply STOP to any campaign text message to unsubscribe. After you opt out, you may receive a confirmation message and no further recurring campaign messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP for help. You may also contact the Campaign at antoniofordistrict5@gmail.com or +1-760-808-1014.

Supported Carriers

Message delivery is subject to your mobile carrier and network availability. Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

Eligibility and Mobile Number Responsibility

You must be authorized to use the mobile number you provide. If your mobile number changes or is reassigned, please opt out or contact the Campaign so campaign records can be updated.

Privacy

The Campaign's handling of personal information and SMS consent is described in the Privacy Policy. The Campaign does not sell or share SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Changes

The Campaign may update these terms as messaging practices or legal requirements change. Updated terms will be posted on this page.
